Less time searching,
more time succeeding.

A free, McMaster-focused platform where students find, share, and rate study materials, organized by course code in one place.

Try the prototypeHow it works

Have you ever spent more time searching for study materials than studying?

Notes, practice questions, and past resources are scattered across group chats, websites, and shared folders. Studying becomes more stressful and time-consuming.

💬

Scattered

Resources live in dozens of group chats and folders that few students can see.

💸

Paid alternatives

Public services can charge fees and aren’t organized around McMaster courses.

❓

Unreliable

Without ratings, it’s hard to know which notes are worth your time.

Prototype: the homepage

Search a course code, browse by resource type, and explore sample materials. Unlock resources with credits, and earn credits by uploading a demo entry.

Credits: 10

Sample data only. This prototype does not store or upload files.

How it works

A credit and rating system can help keep the library growing and useful.

Search by course

Enter a course code and find resources for that course in one place.

Upload to earn credits

Contribute trusted resources and earn credits to unlock materials.

Rate and spend

Students rate materials from one to five stars; ratings inform demo unlock costs.

Review for quality

A future platform should review uploads for quality, course fit, and academic integrity.

Built on trust

Peer feedback highlighted the need for thoughtful management, integrity, and growth.

🛡️

Academic integrity

Plan for checks that flag plagiarism, off-course material, or confidential content, with clear guidance for contributors.

👩‍💻

Student-run team

Volunteer roles for Engineering and Computer Science students, with a formal selection and review process.

🤝

Campus partnerships

Explore partnerships with the MSU and Student Success to promote the platform and support access.

📣

Getting the word out

Workshops, short videos, social media, and links from existing McMaster pages.

Impact and what’s next

Free access to academic resources supports UN SDG 4 (Quality Education) and SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities).

Now: Homepage prototype, peer feedback, and a refined credit and rating concept.
Next: Pilot with McMaster courses and research scalable, responsible content review.
Later: Expand to other universities.
